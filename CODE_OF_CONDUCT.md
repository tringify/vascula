# Code of conduct

Vascula is a small project and we want it to be a pleasant place to work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning so others can follow it.
- Keep discussion on the work: the language, the library and its documentation.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what is merged.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting issues, pull requests or reviews.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes it hard for you to take part, write to
**developers@tringify.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential. We may remove comments, close threads or block accounts that
break these rules.

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and to
any other place where someone represents the project.
